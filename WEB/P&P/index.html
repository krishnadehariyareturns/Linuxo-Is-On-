Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Linuxo may collect and store limited information necessary to provide, maintain, and protect its services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how Linuxo is used, we may store:

  • Server configuration data — Settings required for Linuxo's features.
  • Discord User IDs — These may be used to identify users, apply permissions, prevent abuse, or maintain certain bot features.
  • IP addresses — An IP address may be collected when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, or protecting our services.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and operate Linuxo's features.
  • Store server-specific configurations.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, or misuse.
  • Investigate security issues.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve the service.

3. Data Security

We make reasonable efforts to protect stored information and limit access to it.

However, no online service can guarantee 100% security. We cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or lost due to circumstances outside our reasonable control.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Linuxo develops. Significant changes may be announced through the project's official communication channels.

6. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Linuxo's handling of data, contact the Linuxo development team through the project's official support channel.
